## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 26.12.2025

### Overview

itmo-to-calendar (“the App”) helps users create or update Google Calendar events based on ITMO schedule data. The App is designed to minimize data access and does not collect or store personal information beyond what is necessary to perform its core functionality.

### Google Calendar Data Access

The App uses **restricted Google Calendar scopes** solely to perform the following user-initiated actions:

* Create a new calendar and add events to it, **or**
* Update an existing calendar by removing and re-adding events

The App **does not read, analyze, or use calendar data for any other purpose**.

### Use of Google Calendar Data (Limited Use Disclosure)

The App’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the **Google API Services User Data Policy**, including the **Limited Use** requirements.

Specifically:

* Google Calendar data is used **only** to create, update, or manage calendar events as explicitly requested by the user.
* The App does **not** use Google Calendar data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any secondary purposes.
* The App does **not** sell, share, or transfer Google user data to third parties.
* The App does **not** store Google Calendar data in a separate database.

### Data Storage

The App itself does not maintain persistent storage of user data.

Google Apps Script and Google services may automatically record **operational metadata**, such as execution timestamps, execution counts, duration statistics, and error logs. These logs are used for debugging and reliability and may be visible to the developer within the Google Apps Script or Google Cloud console. The App does not intentionally log personal calendar content.

All data handled by Google services is subject to **Google’s privacy policies**:
[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services other than Google services required to provide its functionality.

### User Control

You may revoke the App’s access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account permissions page.

### Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if the App’s functionality changes. Updates will be reflected with a revised “Last updated” date.

### Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the Issues tab of the repository where the App is published.
